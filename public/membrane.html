What is solved — and what is not

Every void voxel is connected to adjacent void voxels by assumed equal cylindrical throats. Hagen–Poiseuille conductance defines each bond; nodal mass conservation yields the pressure field. The model enforces impermeable solids and calculates water moving between adjacent XY sheets. No visual particles are invented.

The stepwise dye model assumes that the computed pressure-driven water flow stays constant while a passive tracer enters the inlet. Every time step conserves transported dye-equivalent volume and measures when it first reaches the outlet (1% of inlet concentration). A different route can produce a different arrival time. This is not time-resolved Navier–Stokes.

Neglected: actual pore volume, molecular diffusion, mixing, detailed pore geometry, actual throat-size distribution, inertia, entrance losses, turbulence, solid chemistry, temperature evolution, and Navier–Stokes velocity profiles. Numerical error is checked, but the physical model remains a simplified approximation. This is **not** validated permeability or real-world optimization.
