What is solved — and what is not

Every void voxel is connected to adjacent void voxels by assumed equal cylindrical throats. Hagen–Poiseuille conductance defines each bond; nodal mass conservation yields the pressure field. The model enforces impermeable solids and calculates water moving between adjacent XY sheets. No visual particles are invented.

Neglected: detailed pore geometry, actual throat-size distribution, inertia, entrance losses, turbulence, solid chemistry, temperature evolution, and Navier–Stokes velocity profiles. Numerical error is checked, but the physical model remains a simplified approximation. This is **not** validated permeability or real-world optimization.
