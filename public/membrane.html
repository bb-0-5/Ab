Physical assumptions and limits

V10 implicit surface equations reproduce src/simulations/v10_mesh.ts and create solid/void masks in the CFD grid. Impenetrable voxel faces enforce zero normal fluid velocity; the 3D pressure correction acts only in fluid. Walls and solid interfaces are approximately no-slip, and membrane solids are adiabatic. Thermal conductivity through solids, detailed turbulence, electrochemistry and actual pressure-driven throughflow are not yet modelled. This is a sealed, buoyancy-driven cavity, not an open membrane filter.

All work is local in one browser Web Worker. Mask connectivity uses a six-neighbour path test between hot and cold plates. A short transient screen selects a promising geometry; that does not establish a stable optimum. Numerical validation and mesh refinement are still required.
