What this model can and cannot claim

Implemented: non-dimensional Boussinesq vorticity–streamfunction fluid motion within each XY plane; actual finite-difference diffusion of heat and momentum between Z layers; rigid XY walls; heated lower and cooled upper boundaries. The browser runs the calculations inside one Web Worker.

Not implemented: out-of-plane fluid velocity, 3D pressure projection, compressibility, realistic material properties, physical mesh certification or a full Navier–Stokes solution. Two canvases do not remove those physical limits. The “probability” relevance score controls what we examine, not the physics.
